Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you want to know about our project and workflow.

How did you build this site?

We built it using semantic HTML, custom CSS properties, and JavaScript without external frameworks.

How do you collaborate using Git?

We alternate driver and navigator roles, use co-author commit trailers, and push our updates to GitHub.

Does the site remember theme preferences?

Yes! Your dark or light mode preference is saved locally in your browser using localStorage.

Are the contact form inputs validated?

Yes, JavaScript validates your email format and ensures messages are at least 20 characters long before submission.
